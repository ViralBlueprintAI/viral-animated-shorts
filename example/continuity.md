# Shot and continuity table - The Dancing Plague (frames at 30 fps)

Each line: shot, first frame, light, place, who / what. Written before the code; every change of place is a cut.

1. 0, day, grain market (flash-forward hook): the band on the stage, exhausted dancers in front.
2. 146, day, aerial over Strasbourg after a white flash ("You see, in Strasbourg").
3. 197, day, Troffea's street: her door swings open, she looks both ways, steps down, the dance takes her on "dance".
4. 287, day, same street: a shocked neighbour big in the foreground; mother and child, a grumpy man behind her.
5. 333, day, same: close-up, she can't stop.
6. 372, day, same: her husband pleads at her side, sweat drop, points to the door.
7-10. 431-498, day/night swaps, same: six days; the husband falls asleep on the step at night (lit windows).
11. 498, day, same street: thirty neighbours dance with her; crane up.
12. 574, day, aerial over the grain market: four hundred dancers.
13-14. 689 / 716, day / night (torches), among the dancers on the market.
15. 729, day, market: long lens on dancing legs and worn shoes, red marks on the cobbles.
16. 773, day, COUNCIL CHAMBER: the council at the long table; the mayor bangs the gavel on "calls"; doors fly open, the doctors march in.
17. 845, day, CHAMBER: the doctor holds the urine flask up to the window ("Their verdict?"); insert: his eye magnified through the liquid, on "Hot" it boils red.
18. 893, day, CHAMBER: shocked council, the doctor points outside and shows them the jig on "dance it out"; gavel on "out".
19. 953, day, grain market: workers carry the grain sacks away (slung over one shoulder).
20. 996, day, market: three carpenters hammer, the stage pops up behind them.
21. 1025, day, stage: a fat purse flies to the band, gold burst, they start to play.
22. 1064, day, market: professional dancers among the crowd.
23. 1139, day, town hall steps: the council cheers, dancers right below them ("It works perfectly").
24. 1181, day, market wide: the dancing gets worse.
25. 1235, golden, market: a dancer collapses exactly on "death", stars and dust.
26. 1316, golden, market: crane up and away ("So what was making a whole city dance?").
27. 1390, night, A POOR HOME: a family at a bare table, one candle; "Famine": the empty bowl; "disease": the father coughs. Cut at 1496 to the St Vitus shrine in the street.
28. 1556, night, shrine: the statue's eyes glow red, the curse swirl.
29. 1636, night, the street by the shrine: a villager trembles, sweats, and starts to dance.
30. 1806, night, same street: one yawn spreads along a row of tired people.
31. 1884, day, grain market: the council nails up the "no music" board, the band leaves.
32. 1950, day, country road: the dancers walk to the chapel in red shoes (camera alongside).
33. 2005, day, road: red shoes close.
34. 2041, golden, St Vitus chapel: the dancers stand calm ("And it worked").
35. 2064, golden, crane out over the chapel to the city.
